Owning music again, in three moves

1 · Buy the files

Bandcamp sells DRM-free downloads and pays artists directly — most albums cost less than two coffees, and they're yours forever. Qobuz and 7digital work too. Downloads arrive as a .zip: import it here exactly as it is, no unpacking needed.
OPEN BANDCAMP

2 · Raid what you already own

Old CDs rip to MP3 or FLAC with free tools (fre:ac, Exact Audio Copy). Files sitting on an old laptop or drive can be dropped into iCloud/Files and imported from there. Every one becomes a star.

3 · Keep it safe

Everything stays on this device — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or shared. Settings has a one-tap full backup; stash it in your files or cloud drive and your sky can always be re-lit by importing it back.
